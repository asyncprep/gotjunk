## Description

**French (Canada)**

GOT-Crew remplace la feuille de route papier des équipes de camion de 1-800-GOT-JUNK? Montréal.

Le chef d'équipe saisit la route du jour sur l'iPhone du camion :
- les travaux : numéro d'opportunité, montant, taxes, pourboire et mode de paiement;
- les visites au dépotoir, les pesées et la photo du billet;
- le comptant à remettre et sa photo;
- les heures de début et de fin.

L'application calcule en direct les revenus, le revenu par heure, le coût net du dépotoir, la répartition des pourboires et le partage des profits. Elle fonctionne sans réseau et envoie tout dès que le signal revient. La répartition suit la route en direct et l'accepte en fin de journée.

Réservée aux employés invités par la franchise.

**English**

GOT-Crew replaces the paper route sheet for the truck teams of 1-800-GOT-JUNK? Montréal.

The crew lead enters the day's route on the truck's iPhone:
- jobs: opportunity number, amount, tax, tip and payment method;
- dump visits, weigh-ins and the ticket photo;
- the cash to hand in and its photo;
- start and finish times.

The app shows revenue, revenue per hour, net disposal cost, the tip split and the profit share live. It works without signal and sends everything as soon as signal returns. Dispatch follows the route live and accepts it at the end of the day.

For employees invited by the franchise only.

## App Review notes

> GOT-Crew is an internal business app for the employees of the 1-800-GOT-JUNK? Montréal
> franchise, distributed privately through Apple Business Manager. Accounts are created by the
> franchise; there is no public sign-up.
>
> Demo account (crew lead): see the Sign-In Information fields. After signing in, "Route du
> jour" shows a demo route assigned to this account for today. Tap "Commencer la route",
> choose an hours type, then add a job ("Ajouter un travail"), a dump visit and a photo. The
> app works offline and syncs when the network returns.
>
> The camera is used only to photograph dump tickets and the cash handed in.

Sign-in information: a demo crew login, entered by Khalil in App Store Connect (never stored in
the repository). The demo account needs a demo route on each day of the review.
