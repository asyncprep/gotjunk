# Truck Team OS – Crew app guide

Montréal · November 3, 2026

## What the app is

The crew app replaces the paper route sheet. You enter your route on the truck's iPhone during the day: jobs, dumps, cash and times. Dispatch sees it live and accepts it at the end of the day, and the app shows your profit share as you go.

- **One person enters the route: the crew lead**, signed in on the truck's phone. The others can sign in to look, but only the crew lead can change it.
- The app is in French or English: choose on the sign-in screen, or in the menu (⋮) → **Paramètres (Settings)** → **Langue (Language)**. Your choice is saved on your account, so on a shared truck phone the app switches to your language when you sign in.
- It works without signal. Everything is saved on the phone and sent when signal comes back.

## Signing in and finding your route

1. Open **GOT-Crew** on the truck's phone.
2. Sign in with the email and password you set from your invitation.
3. **Route du jour (Today's route)** shows your truck, phone, crew with each person's position (Driver, Navigator or Onboarding) and status.

If you see **No route assigned today**, dispatch hasn't put you on a route for today: call them. If it says another person is the crew lead, you can look but not change anything. If the crew lead has left, or there is none, tap **Prendre la relève (Take over)** to become crew lead on this phone.

At the end of the day, don't sign out while items are still waiting to send: the app warns you, and signing out then would lose them.

## Starting the route

1. Choose the **Type d'heures (Hours type)**: regular junk hours, warehouse hours or non-junk hours. Only regular junk hours earn a profit share.
2. Tap **Commencer la route (Start route)**. The start time is recorded; dispatch can correct it from the tracker.
3. Answer the route questions under **Pour le calcul (For the calculation)** when they apply: fuel (one truck, split day, 2 trucks), **Camions de relève utilisés (Swing trucks used)**, with the number of trucks (+ and −; the fee is charged for each), and **Journée de formation (Training day)** with the hours the trainer removes.

The status changes to **En cours (In progress)** and dispatch sees your route live.

## Jobs and cancellations

Tap **Ajouter un travail (Add a job)** for each job.

**A completed job:**

1. Enter the **Opportunity ID** from Pipeline. Each number can be used only once per route.
2. Choose **Complété (Completed)** and the payment method (credit card, debit, cash, invoice, e-transfer…).
3. Enter the **amount before tax** and the discount if any. **Calculer les taxes (Calculate tax)** fills in the total including tax; check it against the receipt.
4. Enter the **tip**, then **Save**.

**A job that didn't convert:** call dispatch before you leave the client. Then choose **Annulé / pas de vente (Cancelled / no sale)**. The job shows **waiting for dispatch** until dispatch confirms the reason.

The scoreboard at the top updates with each job: revenue before tax, jobs, tips and revenue per hour.

## Dumps, weigh-ins and ticket photos

Under **Dépotoir (Disposal)**, tap **Ajouter une visite (Add a visit)** at each dump or depot.

1. Pick the **site**.
2. Enter the **ticket number** and **Prendre une photo (Take a photo)** of the ticket when the site gives one. A ticket number can be used only once at a site.
3. Tap **Ajouter la pesée (Add weigh-in)** for each material (garbage, wood, bedload, metal and electronics, donations…) with its weight in kg.

Each weigh-in shows an estimate from the site's rate: a cost for garbage, wood and bedload, a credit for metal and accepted donations. **Net disposal** is the total costs minus credits. If the app says there is no rate for a material at that site, tell dispatch.

## Cash, tips and profit share

**Cash.** The app adds up your cash jobs and shows **Comptant à remettre (Cash to hand in)**. Before you submit, lay the cash out and take the **Photo du comptant (Cash photo)**. Dispatch counts the envelope at the end of the day.

**Tips.** **Pourboires par personne (Tips per person)** shows each person's share. Tips are split equally among the crew for the time each person was on the route; dispatch can change the split.

**Profit share.** The **Partage des profits (Profit share)** card works like the bottom of the paper sheet, live during the day:

- whether the route **qualifies**, is **not qualified yet**, or **doesn't qualify**, and what is missing (for example "Revenue: $1,600 of $2,000 minimum");
- revenue per hour, team hours, average job size and COGS %;
- each person's % and amount, based on their rank and the COGS band;
- **How it's calculated:** wages (team hours × rate), dumps (costs − credits), franchise fees, fuel and swing truck.

Hours count up to now while the route is going, so the numbers move during the day. The final amount is the one on the route dispatch accepts.

## Submitting, no signal, and returned routes

**Submit at the end of the day.**

1. Tap **Terminer la route (Finish route)** and check the finish time.
2. The app lists anything to fix first (a missing ticket photo, cash photo, ticket number, hours type, an incomplete job…). Fix each one.
3. Tap **Soumettre la route (Submit route)** and confirm. After that you can't change it unless dispatch returns it.

**No signal.** The bar at the top says **Hors ligne (Offline)** and how many changes are waiting. Keep working: everything is saved on the phone and sent, photos included, as soon as signal comes back. Open the app with signal before signing out so nothing is left behind.

**Returned for correction.** If dispatch returns the route, the phone shows **Retourné (Returned)** with their reason. Fix what they asked and submit again.

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Only the crew lead can change this route | Ask the crew lead, or **Take over** if they left |
| This opportunity number is already on this route | Check the number; each job has its own |
| No active rate for this material at this site | Tell dispatch; they add the rate |
| A crew member is already on another active route that day | Call dispatch |
| The route is being reviewed by dispatch | It's submitted; wait for dispatch to accept or return it |
